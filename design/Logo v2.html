Logo v2 - contrast rework

v1 relied on 25-35% opacity satellites and a var(--text) hub: the faint dots vanish on the light theme, and the hub flips meaning between themes. Every v2 variant keeps the clock-hands metaphor but uses only solid accent shapes, so the mark reads identically on any background and at 26px.

2a — ring hub

Thicker hands, no satellites, hub is an accent ring: the background shows through the middle, so the mark has "air" and stays theme-neutral.
