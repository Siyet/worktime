Pinned running timers: the compact strip

The owner's complaint: once the Running card sticks to the top it keeps its full layout - heading, a project/tags line per row, card padding - and with the 2-6 agent timers that are normal here it covers a third of a phone screen while showing only two and a half of them. This page is the decided design for the stuck state, the alternatives that lost, and the spec.

One fixed-height line per timer.Project dot, task title (one line, end ellipsis), ticking h:mm:ss, an icon Stop. No heading, no project/tags line, no card padding. 44px lines on touch (Stop 44×44), 32px with a mouse. Stop comes with an 8-second Undo.

Editing stays one tap away.Tapping a title opens the entry editor (as today) - project and tags live there. The quick project/tags menus stay in the full card at the top of the page, which is now uncapped.

Revision 2 - after engineering review
  1. Structural zero footprint: zero-height sticky box, strip drawn above it; no negative margin, no measured height.
  2. The strip grows only when timers start or stop; +N and group sessions are an overlay below it.
  3. Sync-started timer while shown: carry the reader's row if idle ≥ 250ms, else overlay. A stop never scrolls.
  4. Fixed line heights: 44px touch (Stop 44×44), 32px mouse. All numbers updated.
  5. Stop has Undo on the existing 8-second toast; agent rows need a server change (engineering note).
  6. 8h+: amber time; a hidden one tints the +N line and adds "· 1 over 8h". No reordering.
  7. Identical truncated titles fall back to the session tag, then the start time.
  8. Line cap re-evaluated on width/rotation only, never on toolbar collapse.
  9. Swap a11y: hidden rendering goes inert at the start of the swap; open quick menus close.
  10. Decided: full card uncapped; group time = tracked total; cap is a fixed rule.

Phone, 360px, dark - today vs proposed, stuck

The page is scrolled into the feed; the pinned block sits at the top edge and the feed runs under it.

Phone, 390px, light

Light uses the same tokens; two values are new. The strip's shadow is 0 6px 18px rgba(20,26,40,.12) (today's rgba(0,0,0,.28) reads as a smudge on the light background), and the 8h+ amber is color-mix(accent 60%, text), because plain --accent on white is 2.9:1.

Desktop, 1200px (frames at 50%)

Same one-line rows at 32px. The width that a phone does not have carries the project name after the title (dim, and it gives way before the title does). Up to six timers stay visible when the window is at least 50rem tall.

Transition: hand-off

The full card is an ordinary card in the flow and scrolls away. Right after it sits a zero-height sticky box with top: calc(env(safe-area-inset-top) + H); the strip is drawn above the box (position: absolute; bottom: 0). The box sticks when its static top - the card's bottom edge - reaches H below the top edge, which is exactly when the strip covers the card's last H px. The box's top is the stuck edge and the reading line. H comes from the line count, not a measurement; a stale H would only shift the strip a couple of px, never layout. The two renderings swap with visibility.

Rejected: collapse in place

Why the sticky card must not change its own height. Collapsing it at the stick point shortens the flow above the feed by Δ = full − compact (100-150px). Either the feed jumps Δ under the reader's thumb (above), or the scroller compensates with an instant scrollTo(scrollY − Δ). That cancels iOS momentum scrolling, and whenever the reader is less than Δ past the stick point it pulls scrollY back across the threshold: the card unsticks, grows, and the next frame sticks it again. The browser's own scroll anchoring is exactly such a compensation - with it left on (the app turns it off), this mode flipped on its own while this page was being built. Also rejected: overlay from the first pixel - for the whole Δ of scroll every timer is on screen twice, each with two focusable Stops.

Six timers, 360px, stuck. Constraints: every timer identifiable (title + colour), elapsed visible, one-tap Stop, as little height as possible.

Behaviour

  1. Two renderings, exactly one live. The full card keeps today's content (heading, project combobox, tags, Stop), is the editing surface, and is no longer sticky, capped or shadowed. The strip is the pinned view. Their expansion states are separate.
  2. Zero footprint by structure. The box is zero-height; the strip is absolutely positioned above it. The swap, the overlay and a stale H can never change layout. H only decides where the strip sits at the moment of the swap.
  3. Stuck ⇔ the box's rendered top is below its static top, i.e. the card's bottom edge has reached H below the top edge. This is FeedScroller's existing test with pinned = the box and sentinel = a zero-height marker just before it.
  4. The swap. Stick: the full card becomes inert immediately, any open quick menu (project, tags, row menu) in it closes, focus hands off, the strip fades in over 120ms while the card stays visible beneath it, then the card gets visibility: hidden. Unstick: the same in reverse - the strip goes inert at once, its overlay collapses, the card is live immediately and the strip fades out. No height, position or transform animation. prefers-reduced-motion: an instant swap.
  5. The strip's height changes only when timers start or stop. +N and group sessions open in the overlay below it; opening, closing or scrolling the overlay never changes H, the reading line or the scroll padding.
  6. A timer starts while the strip is shown (sync, Undo, Repeat): the strip grows one line. If the reader has been idle for 250ms (no scroll, wheel, touch or scroll key - FeedScroller's existing IDLE_MS), the reader's row is carried down with the strip's bottom edge, as today. Otherwise nothing is scrolled - the strip overlays one more line of feed, so a fling is never cancelled.
  7. A timer stops while the strip is shown: the strip shrinks one line and nothing on screen moves - the scroller absorbs the full card's height change above the viewport as it does today, and the strip simply uncovers the row beneath. The last timer stopped: box and card disappear together.
  8. Stop and Undo. Stop acts at once (no confirm) and shows the shell's toast: Stopped · title · Undo · ✕ for 8 seconds, the deleteEntryWithUndo pattern (one pending undo; a newer stop or delete replaces it; in-app navigation keeps it). Undo restores the same row with stopped_at = null, so it keeps running from its original start; the restored line follows rule 6. Agent rows get Undo too, which needs the server change in the engineering notes; without it they get none.
  9. 8 hours and more. Elapsed turns amber in place. If such a timer is behind +N, the +N line is tinted and appends · 1 over 8h (t("{n} over 8h")). Rows are never reordered for it. A group counts when any of its sessions has run 8 hours.
  10. Identical truncated titles. When two visible rows' titles come out the same after truncation (compared once per render and per width change: the visible prefix that fits the title cell, measured with the row font), those rows show a suffix that never truncates: the session tag if the row is an agent row, else the start time (formatTime). Rows that are unambiguous stay clean.
  11. Line cap. Evaluated when the viewport width changes or the device rotates - never on a height-only resize, which on a phone is the toolbar collapsing mid-scroll. The height it reads is 100svh, which toolbars do not change. With a mouse (no collapsing toolbars) any resize re-evaluates.
  12. Editing. Tap the title → the entry editor, exactly as the full card's title does today. The inline project/tags menus stay in the full card only.
  13. Order. Same as the full card (newest start first), so spatial memory survives the swap; the rows behind +N are the longest-running ones.

Accessibility

  1. Structure. <section aria-label="Running"> (a region landmark) holding a <ul>: "list, 3 items". The heading is dropped visually only.
  2. Stop. A real <button> whose accessible name is exactly t("Stop") (aria-label) - getByRole("button", { name: "Stop" }) keeps working - plus aria-describedby pointing at the row's title: "Stop, button, AIM V2 epic review".
  3. Titles. Accessible name = title [+ suffix] + ", Project" (sr-only), so colour never carries the project alone. Group lines add "2 entries" and Expand/Collapse.
  4. Overlay. +N and group lines have aria-expanded and aria-controls. Escape collapses the overlay and keeps focus on its toggle; unsticking collapses it (focus, if inside, returns to the toggle's twin via the hand-off).
  5. The swap. The rendering being hidden is inert from the first frame of the swap and visibility: hidden after the fade: out of the tab order, the accessibility tree and Playwright's role queries. At the top of the page only the full card's controls exist, so existing e2e selectors keep working.
  6. Focus hand-off, both ways. Before making a rendering inert, move focus (preventScroll: true) to its twin: same entry, same role; project/tags triggers go to the title; a row now behind +N goes to the +N button.
  7. After Stop. When Stop was activated from the keyboard or a screen reader (a click with detail === 0), focus moves to the control that took the stopped line's place - the next line's Stop or group toggle, else the line above, else +N; with no timers left, the start form's description - never to body. The toast is role="status", so "Stopped, title" is announced, exactly as for delete today.
  8. Order and targets. Focus order = visual order: title, Stop, per line, then +N, then the overlay (when open), then the feed. Stop 44×44 on touch; the title fills the rest of its line; toggles are full width × 44.
  9. Contrast. Title 12.3:1 dark / 14.6:1 light; dim text 5.2 / 4.7; late time 7.5 / 5.4 (4.9 on the tint); tinted +N label 10.4 / 13.3. The Stop face border is decorative - the glyph identifies it. --accent focus rings on white are 2.9:1, a system-wide token issue not introduced here.

Edge cases

  • Long titles - one line, end ellipsis; full text in the tooltip, the editor and the accessible name. Never two lines: fixed line height is what makes H arithmetic.
  • ru / de / fr / es / zh - no translated text in a row except the new strings: t("+{n} more") (+3 more, ещё 3, 3 weitere, 3 de plus, 3 más, 还有 3 个), t("{n} over 8h"), t("Stopped").
  • Hours ≥ 10 or ≥ 100 - 8 or 9 characters; that row's time column grows, the Stop column does not move.
  • Group time - the tracked total, matching the full card; sessions show their own.
  • Undo after the row changed elsewhere - Undo writes like any edit (LWW); if the entry was deleted or edited on another device in the meantime, the latest version wins, as for delete-undo today.
  • No timers - neither rendering exists. Offline - nothing changes; elapsed and Undo are local writes.
  • A page too short for the card to leave - the strip never shows; nothing to guard.
  • Safe area - the strip sits under env(safe-area-inset-top), like today's card.

Engineering notes

  • FeedScroller. Keeps its logic: pinned() returns the box, sentinel() the marker. The reading line is the box's top; its getBoundingClientRect().bottom, which FeedScroller already reads, is the same value because the box has no height. The negative scroll-margin-top for the card's controls moves to the strip's. No bind:offsetHeight or ResizeObserver is involved in H.
  • Floating menus. With the full card uncapped and never pinned, the running rows' menus no longer sit in a clipping scroller: floatOutOfClip (lib/float-menu.ts) becomes unnecessary for them.
  • Undo on agent rows - server change in the same release. Undo is offered on agent rows too - most strip lines are agent rows, so that is where mis-taps land. It requires a server change shipped in the same release: the sync write that restarts an agent row (stopped_at or deleted_at back to null) settles it in the same transaction - not on the next heartbeat, because a session that goes quiet would leave the row running forever (reconciliation only walks active sessions). The rule runs once per row after the whole push is written, so undoing several stops in one push settles the same in any row order, rows of one session in start order. If the server itself ended the row (an idle split, the stop hook, reconciliation - recorded in the server-only agent_end), that end is put back and nothing else changes. If the user ended it: with nothing of the session after it, the session takes it back - still running for a live session, closed at the later of the user's stop and the session's end for an ended one; a replacement the session opened within the idle threshold and nobody touched is tombstoned and the session takes the row back; a replacement that was edited (or is written by the same push) keeps running and keeps the session, and the restored row is closed where the replacement begins, so the two never overlap; anything later than that (a replacement past the idle threshold, several later rows) leaves the restored row closed again at the user's stop. Without that server change, agent rows get no Undo. Tests cover every order: Undo before the next heartbeat; a heartbeat before Undo, with an untouched and an edited replacement; the stop hook and reconciliation before Undo; a late offline push after a server split; and one Undo restarting two rows of the same session.
  • Toast buttons on touch. Undo must be 44px on coarse pointers. Today .toast button { min-height: 2.4rem } (web/src/app.css ~597) beats the touch rule button { min-height: 2.75rem } (~869) on specificity, so the real Undo button is 38px; the coarse block needs a .toast button override.
  • Page keys. FeedScroller's page-key handler decides whether the pinned card can still scroll its own rows by looking at pinned.firstElementChild. With the box and strip that is the strip, which never scrolls; the check must look at the open +N/group overlay's scroller instead (and let the page scroll when no overlay is open).
  • e2e. Specs working at the top of the page see only the full card, as today. The feed spec's pinned-card height and position assertions will see the box and strip.
  • design/pages/timer.html is left alone: it is a frozen snapshot that already predates tags, kebabs and groups.

Decided after review

  • Full card height cap - dropped: it no longer pins, so at the top of the page every timer shows in full with no scroll inside a scroll.
  • Group time - the tracked total.
  • Line cap - the fixed 2 / 4 / 6 rule, no setting.
  • Forgotten timers - amber at 8h, surfaced on the +N line, never reordered.
