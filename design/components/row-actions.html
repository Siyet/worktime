Decided: native <dialog> with showModal() - bottom sheet below 34rem, centred 32rem card above, one markup block. Not inline. The sheet grows upward so the focused field stays above the mobile keyboard and Save stays in the thumb zone, and the native dialog gives focus trap, Escape, ::backdrop and background inert with no JS.

The "see the neighbours" argument for inline editing is answered better inside the dialog: a live boundary line states the previous end, the gap and the next start, with an overlaps 20m danger variant. That is the actual information wanted when fixing a time, and it is precise instead of visual.

Running entry: the full editor, with To empty and a dim running placeholder. Typing a time stops it retroactively - "I forgot to stop at 17:00" is the main reason to open a running entry. Stop now fills the current time. To can never be cleared: toReportEntries skips stopped_at === null, so a clearable To would silently delete hours from every report, the CSV and the printed sheet. If the row is stopped on another device while the editor is open, the editor swaps to the finished layout with a notice instead of saving its stale snapshot.

Time input: free-text mono, inputmode="numeric", always 24h. Accepts 9, 930, 0930, 9:30, 9.30 and normalises on blur; ArrowUp/Down nudge a minute, Shift+Arrow fifteen. Unparseable sets aria-invalid, a danger border, — in the readout and disables Save. input[type=time] was rejected: it follows the browser locale instead of this app's own hourCycle setting, refuses 930, and cannot express an end date. The +Nd stepper is what makes a 23:40-00:20 entry representable at all - without it validateChanges rejects the whole batch on stopped_at < started_at.
