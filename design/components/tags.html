Decided: no per-tag hue, ever. Hue in an entry row already means project - 15 of them, user-picked - and teal, purple and danger are bound to vacation, dayoff and sick on Time off and in the DailyChart legend. A third colour axis on the same row informs nothing. A tag is read, not decoded. --accent appears only as the selected state, which is what it already means in .seg button.on.

Absence is a shape, not a colour: untagged is dashed and italic, so it survives greyscale print and colour-vision deficiency. The rejected first cut used color-mix(in srgb, var(--tag) 72%, var(--text)), which put green at roughly 3:1 in the light theme - under AA for 12px text.

Tag picker - a toggle grid, not a popover

review development meeting analysis other

Decided: a flat toggle grid, because the picker lives inside a modal editor and a popover inside a dialog is a stacking-context fight for nothing. Names are normalised on create - trimmed, whitespace-collapsed, lowercased, 24 chars - and matched case-insensitively, so typing Review toggles the existing review instead of forking it.

Creation lives here because tagging has to be a sub-second offline action. Rename, merge and remove-from-all-entries live in Settings, each behind a count-bearing confirm - six years of on-the-fly creation guarantees near-duplicates that silently fragment a report group, and with names-as-values those three operations are the only repair tool.

Decided: split, not count-each-fully. An entry with k tags contributes billable(entry)/k to each group; weights sum to exactly 1 under every grouping, so the header total does not depend on Group by. Detail rows print their weighted share with a dim 1/k marker, so a reader who adds the detail rows gets the group header exactly - today ReportsPage.svelte:463 prints the full duration there, which under a tag group would show 1h under a 30m header.

untagged is a first-class group and a first-class filter chip, mirroring NO_PROJECT_KEY. Without a key of its own, roughly a third of the real entries would match no active tag chip and silently vanish from the totals, the chart and the CSV. It stays distinct from the user tag other: "I classified this as other" and "I never tagged this" are different facts.
